# Product specification

## Positioning
A managed AI receptionist platform sold by an operator to local businesses. The operator finds prospects, creates a proposal, configures a receptionist, sets a monthly client price, tests the line, and launches it.

## Page narrative
1. Show a phone call being answered.
2. Show the same call captured in a business portal.
3. Explain that the operator sells the service while the platform runs the infrastructure.
4. Walk through prospect discovery, need scoring, proposal, configuration, pricing, and go-live.
5. Explain architecture, memory, and continuity.
6. Let the visitor test estimated margin with price and usage sliders.
7. Present the six-stage system flow.
8. Present pricing, comparison, FAQ, and closing CTA.

## Functional areas
- Responsive sticky header and mobile menu.
- Hero call scene with phone and call outcome cards.
- Portal dashboard mockup.
- Six-step workflow with active preview.
- Infrastructure cards.
- Profit calculator: price range 150–800 by 10; usage range 250–1500 by 25; usage cost = minutes × 0.30; margin = price − usage cost.
- Pricing plan selector: Starter, Growth, Empire.
- Monthly/annual billing selector.
- FAQ disclosure list.
- Footer navigation.

## Routes and anchors
The initial implementation is a single-page app. Keep these anchors stable: `#top`, `#how`, `#calculator`, `#pricing`, `#faq`, `#close`.
